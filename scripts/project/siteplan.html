TechBridge IT Solutions

Site Name

TechBridge IT Solutions

The name TechBridge IT Solutions represents a company that connects small businesses and startups with reliable technology services. “TechBridge” communicates the idea of bridging the gap between business needs and practical IT solutions.

Optional domain: techbridge-it.com

Site Purpose

The site will introduce professional IT services for small businesses and startups. It will provide information about Microsoft 365 administration, cloud solutions, cybersecurity, endpoint management, and technical support. Visitors will be able to compare services, save services that interest them, and use a contact form to request information or an IT consultation.

Scenarios

  • What IT services can help my small business improve security and productivity?
  • Which service is best for managing and protecting my company’s computers and mobile devices?
  • How can I contact TechBridge to request an IT consultation?

Typography

Montserrat

Montserrat will be used for the site name, headings, navigation, and buttons.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, lists, form labels, and other body content.

Planned Site Structure

  • Home: Introduction, featured services, benefits, and a consultation call to action.
  • Services: Service cards, category filters, and saved services.
  • Contact: Company contact information and consultation request form.
  • References: Sources for external text and images used on the site.
